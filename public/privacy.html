Privacy Policy

This notice describes how S-Cloud handles information when you use its publisher and download services.

1. Information processed

  • Account details, such as your name, email, login information and account settings.
  • File information you submit, including Google Drive links or IDs, names, sizes, types, share links and mirror status.
  • Technical and usage information, including IP addresses, login activity, device or browser information where available, and download and earnings records.
  • Withdrawal or payment details you provide when using payout features.

2. How information is used

We use information to provide and improve our services, manage accounts and file sharing, administer earnings and payments, respond to support requests, and protect the service from fraud and misuse.

3. External providers

We may share information with third-party hosting, file-sharing, and service providers as necessary to operate S-Cloud. These providers may handle information under their own privacy policies and may retain files or records independently of S-Cloud.

4. Cookies and sessions

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, maintain your preferences, and protect the service against abuse. Authorized support staff may access account information when necessary to assist you. External services may use their own cookies.

5. Retention and deletion

Information is retained as needed to run the service, prevent abuse, maintain financial records and resolve disputes. Removing a S-Cloud link does not necessarily remove copies or records held by external providers. Do not submit sensitive or confidential files intended to remain private.

6. Security and your choices

You can manage your profile and sharing settings and remove eligible file records through your account. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials private and report suspected unauthorized access.

7. Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete your information, contact the S-Cloud operator through our official Telegram channel for contact information. This notice may be updated as the service changes.
