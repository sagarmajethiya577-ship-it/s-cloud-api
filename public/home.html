Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Important information before uploading your first file.

Is S-Cloud free to use?

You can create an account and use the available upload and sharing features within your account limits.

How many files can I upload?

The default daily upload limit and per-folder upload limit are 20 files each. Limits may vary according to your account settings.

When will my download mirrors be ready?

Mirror requests are processed in the background. Availability depends on the enabled providers, source access and their processing times.

Can I make my original Drive file private after upload?

Keep the original file accessible until the providers finish importing it. A returned mirror link does not always mean the file transfer has completed.

How do publisher earnings work?

Earnings depend on your account's monetization settings and eligible activity. New accounts start with a zero CPM; earning rates are not guaranteed.
